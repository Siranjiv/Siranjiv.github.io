Cemented Dreams — Project Overview

Cemented Dreams is an MFA Advanced Game Project (AGP), a large-scale, studio-modeled production built by a multidisciplinary team. The game takes place in a brutalist world where architecture is alive, sentient, and hungry. In this society, humans are extracted, processed, and circulated into a single substance: vodka, a fuel pure enough to power the towering structures that rule the landscape. The architecture moves when commanded. It feeds when ordered.

I worked on Cemented Dreams as the Primary Engineer, contributing to graphics programming, gameplay systems, and internal tools, with a secondary role as a Game Designer. My technical work was done in Unreal Engine using C++, where I developed core systems, built tools to support the team, and implemented features that brought the world’s unsettling mechanics to life.

Cemented Dreams followed the structure of USC’s Advanced Games Projects: a long-form, collaborative development cycle designed to mirror real industry production. Our team functioned like a small studio, working in sprints, presenting regular milestones, responding to publishing-style faculty reviews, and coordinating across designers, artists, writers, engineers, and audio collaborators. This environment challenged me to write maintainable code, support cross-discipline workflows, communicate clearly, and contribute meaningfully to a shared creative vision.

Throughout the project, I helped expand the game’s technical foundation while also contributing to its worldbuilding through systems design. Cemented Dreams gave me the opportunity to work through the full lifecycle of a large-scale collaborative game project, from ongoing development and iteration to a completed production.
